Mizani - Privacy Policy

Mizani is a personal budget app. All your data is stored on your own device. If you turn on sync, the app uses the Google Drive hidden app data folder (scope drive.appdata) in YOUR Google account to keep your devices in step.

  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • The app can only see its own hidden folder, never your other Drive files.
  • You can disconnect at any time inside the app, or revoke access from your Google Account permissions page.

Mizani's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
